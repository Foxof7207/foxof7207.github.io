GILLY SMP - CODE OF CONDUCT

Be fair and respect the other players. These rules apply on the server, in chat and on Discord.

1. NO CHEATING
   Cheats are not allowed. An anti-cheat runs on the server, and mods such as X-ray, freecam or Wurst are banned.
   Suspected players may be asked to run a verification mod.

2. PVP
   PvP is allowed, as long as it is fair.
   - No teleport-trapping.
   - No spawn-killing.
   - Teleports have a 3-second warmup. Moving or taking damage cancels it, so you can't teleport out of a fight.

3. NO GRIEFING OR STEALING
   Do not destroy, steal from or take advantage of other players' builds or items.
   - First offence: a warning.
   - Second offence: a temporary ban.

4. RESPECT OTHER PLAYERS' BUILDS
   There is no land claiming. If you find a build that is not yours, leave it alone.

5. CHAT AND SPAM
   Be respectful in chat and on Discord. Spam is detected and muted automatically.

6. STAFF DECISIONS
   Staff decide how these rules are applied. To contact staff or appeal a ban, open a ticket on Discord (fastest).

Full command list and details: https://craft.gillyb.net/wiki.html#rules
