# Minecraft Ender Chest, Homes and Server Ranks: How Storage Works on a Survival Server

On a survival server, storage and homes decide how comfortably you can build. This guide covers the ender chest, homes and the rank perks that expand them. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

## Ender chest

### How big is a Minecraft ender chest and can it be upgraded?

On MatchaSMP the ender chest starts at 3 rows (27 slots) and goes up to 6 rows (54 slots) with a rank. Open it anywhere with `/ec`.

### Can I share my ender chest with another player in Minecraft?

Yes. `/ec invite <player>` gives access, `/ec uninvite <player>` removes it, and `/ec invitelist` shows who has it. Invited players open it with `/ecshare <owner>`.

### What can't I store in a Minecraft ender chest?

On MatchaSMP, bedrock, command blocks and barriers can't be stored in the ender chest. The ender chest is backed up automatically every night.

## Homes

### How many homes can I set in Minecraft?

On MatchaSMP you get 2 homes, with more available by rank. Use `/sethome <name>` where you stand, `/home <name>` to go back and `/homes` to list them.

### How do I make a Minecraft home public for other players?

Use `/edithome` to make a home public. Others list public homes with `/phomes` and visit one with `/phome <owner>+<home>`, for example `/phome Steve+shop`.

## Ranks

### What do the ranks on a Minecraft survival server unlock?

MatchaSMP has three ranks, Bronze, Gold and Diamond, and each includes the perks below it. Ender chest rows go from 3 on the default to 6 on Diamond, homes from 2 to 6, and active orders from 5 to 15. Ranks are a quality-of-life perk, not a power advantage.

### What happens to my ender chest if I lose my rank?

Your items stay in the ender chest. You can only reach the rows your current rank allows.

### How do I get a rank on a Minecraft server?

On MatchaSMP, ranks are granted by staff. Have a look at the store and ask in Discord.
