Minecraft Recipe Book and Advancement Guide for Bedrock and Java

This survival rework replaces many vanilla recipes and advancements, and Bedrock can't show all of them in game. These answers explain where to look and how to follow progression on any platform. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

8 answersFull wiki →

Finding recipes

Why is my Bedrock recipe book missing recipes?

Bedrock's recipe book only shows crafting-table recipes. For everything else, such as the Mud Kiln, Oven, Blast Furnace, Kindling and Smithing Table, use the Recipe Book on the MatchaSMP site, which also shows which vanilla item each custom item looks like on Bedrock.

Where can I see every recipe of a custom Minecraft survival rework?

The Recipe Book lists every recipe of the MatchaSMP survival rework and is searchable. Tap an item to see how to make it and what it's used in. It contains spoilers, so use it when you're stuck.

How many recipes does this Minecraft survival rework add?

The rework adds about 1275 recipes and 880 advancements, and it replaces many vanilla advancements and recipes with its own.

Following advancements

How do I follow advancements on Bedrock in Minecraft?

Bedrock can't open the advancements menu, so use the Advancement Guide on the MatchaSMP site. It lists every step in order, what completes it and its rewards, and you can tick steps off as you go.

What is the Angler's Almanac in Minecraft?

The Angler's Almanac is the fish list included in the Advancement Guide, so you can see which fish are part of the progression.

Why won't some recipes show up in my recipe book yet?

On MatchaSMP the recipe book unlocks recipes when you need them, as you progress. If a recipe is missing, check your advancements first, then look it up in the Recipe Book.

What are advancements in a Minecraft survival rework?

On MatchaSMP advancements are the step-by-step progression: each one lists what completes it and its rewards. The Advancement Guide shows the same steps in order for players who can't open the in-game menu.

What should I do first in a Minecraft survival rework?

Open your advancements with L on Java, since they are a step-by-step guide. Early game is slower than vanilla and natural regeneration is off, so take your time at each stage.
