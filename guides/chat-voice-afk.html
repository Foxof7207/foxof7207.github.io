Minecraft Server Chat Guide: Global Chat, Private Messages, Voice Chat and AFK

Chat, voice and AFK rules shape how a server feels, especially on a crossplay server where not everyone uses voice. These answers cover the basics of talking, messaging and idling. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

8 answersFull wiki →

Chat

Is Minecraft server chat global or local?

On MatchaSMP chat is global: everyone sees your messages. There is no ranged chat and no need to shout with !. Your rank shows in front of your name.

How do I send a private message on a Minecraft server?

Use /msg <player> <message>, or /w, /tell, /m or /whisper, and reply with /r. /ignore <player> hides someone and /unignore undoes it.

Can I use colours in Minecraft chat?

Yes. & colour codes work in chat, on signs and in anvil names of up to 32 characters. A message starting with > is green, and one starting with * gets a purple-to-blue gradient.

How is spam handled in Minecraft server chat?

On MatchaSMP, repeated messages, flooding, very long messages, excessive capitals and random characters are detected automatically. Spammers are muted for a short time, and longer if they keep doing it.

Voice chat

How does Minecraft voice chat work on a server?

On MatchaSMP, Java players can install the Simple Voice Chat mod matching Minecraft 26.3, then press V to set it up. You hear players within 48 blocks, or 24 when whispering, and can form groups with G. Recording is disabled.

Is voice chat required to play on a crossplay Minecraft server?

No. Bedrock and console can't use voice chat, so it's never required to play on MatchaSMP.

AFK and coordinates

What happens when you go AFK on a Minecraft server?

On MatchaSMP, after 5 minutes without moving you're marked [AFK] and your name turns grey; /afk toggles it. After 10 more minutes you're disconnected. If the server restarts while you're away, you land in the AFK zone and can return with /server smp.

How do I show my coordinates in Minecraft?

Use /ch toggle (or /coordshud) to show your XYZ position, facing direction and a 24-hour in-game clock in your action bar.
