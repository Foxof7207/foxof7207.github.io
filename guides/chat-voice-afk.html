Gilly SMP Global Chat, Voice Chat, AFK and Bedrock Menu Guide

On Gilly SMP, chat, voice and AFK are all built for a friendly, casual survival world shared by Java, Bedrock and console players. This guide covers global chat and private messages, colour codes and spam mutes, the Java-only Simple Voice Chat mod, the 5-minute AFK timer, the touch-friendly Bedrock /menu and which player commands are enabled.

Global chat & private messages

Who can see my chat messages on Gilly SMP? Is chat global?

Everyone sees your messages: chat on Gilly SMP is global, with no ranged chat and no need to shout with !. Your rank appears in front of your name, for example [Gold] <Steve> hello. Just type normally and the whole server reads it.

How do I send a private message to another player on Gilly SMP?

Use /msg <player> <message> to send a private message; /w, /tell, /m and /whisper all do the same thing. Reply to the last person with /r (also /reply). On Bedrock you can pick the player from /menu → Private message.

How do I ignore or block someone in chat on Gilly SMP?

Run /ignore <player> to hide someone's messages, and /block works too. Undo it with /unignore or /unblock.

How do I send an emote in chat on Gilly SMP?

Use /me <action>, also available as /emote. For example, /me waves displays in chat as * Steve waves.

Can I use colour codes and special formatting in Gilly SMP chat?

Yes, & colour codes work in chat, on signs and in anvil names (up to 32 characters). A message starting with > is shown in green and the > stays visible. A message starting with * becomes a purple-to-blue gradient.

Spam, voice chat & AFK

What happens if I spam in chat on Gilly SMP?

Spam is detected automatically: repeating the same message, flooding, very long messages, excessive capitals and random characters all trigger it. Spammers are muted for a short time, and the mute lasts longer if they keep doing it.

How do I use voice chat on Gilly SMP?

Java players can install the Simple Voice Chat mod (Fabric, NeoForge, Forge or Quilt) for Minecraft 26.3 and press V to set it up once joined. You hear players within 48 blocks, or 24 blocks when whispering, and can make groups with G. Push-to-talk is on Caps Lock by default, with voice activation available in the settings, and recording is disabled. Bedrock and console players can't use it, so voice chat is never required.

Can Bedrock or console players use voice chat on Gilly SMP?

No. The Simple Voice Chat proximity mod is Java-only, so Bedrock and console players can't use it and it's never required to play.

What happens when I go AFK on Gilly SMP?

After 5 minutes without moving you're marked [AFK] and your name turns grey; /afk toggles it manually. After 10 more minutes you're disconnected to keep the server fast, and you simply rejoin. If the main server restarts while you're away, you land in the AFK zone and can come back with /server smp.

Bedrock menu & commands

How do I open the menu on Bedrock on Gilly SMP?

Type /menu to open a touch-friendly menu with buttons for the most common commands: Spawn, Homes, Teleport requests, Ender Chest, Orders, Trade, Private message, Points, Referrals, My rank and Tools. Whenever you need to pick a player, you get a list of everyone online. Java players don't need the menu and use the commands directly.

Why are some buttons grey in the Gilly SMP Bedrock menu?

Buttons that need another player are greyed out with "nobody else is online" when you're alone, and "Go to a home" is greyed out until you set a home. Clickable chat messages don't work on Bedrock, so use /menu to accept or decline teleport requests and to pick homes. You can see your rank and every rank's limits under /menu → My rank.

What does "That command is not available" mean on Gilly SMP?

Only player commands are enabled on Gilly SMP; anything else is blocked, hidden from tab completion and answers "That command is not available. Bedrock players: use /menu." The full list of allowed commands is in All commands, covering chat, homes, teleporting, economy, storage and tools.
