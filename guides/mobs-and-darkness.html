Why Are There So Few Monsters Outside? Mobs, Phantoms and True Darkness in Minecraft

This survival rework changes which monsters appear in the open, removes phantoms and uses true darkness. These answers explain what spawns where, how sleeping works and when the surface stops being dangerous. Gilly SMP is the worked example.

8 answersFull wiki →

Monsters and spawns

Why don't creepers spawn in the open in Minecraft?

On Gilly SMP, creepers, spiders, endermen, witches and slimes don't spawn in the open Overworld. Zombies, skeletons and other undead still spawn at night, so caves and ruins are where the real danger is.

Do endermen and witches spawn in the open in Minecraft?

Not on Gilly SMP. Endermen, witches and slimes don't spawn in the open Overworld, the same as creepers and spiders.

Where is the real danger in Minecraft survival?

On Gilly SMP, caves and ruins are where the real danger is. Zombies and skeletons still spawn in the open at night, so bring a weapon and light.

Are phantoms disabled in this Minecraft survival rework?

Yes. Phantoms are disabled on Gilly SMP, so staying up at night doesn't bring them after you.

When is the surface safe in Minecraft survival?

On Gilly SMP, once the Ender Dragon is defeated for the first time, evil is banished from the surface for everyone. Nothing hostile spawns in the open any more.

Darkness and sleeping

What is true darkness in Minecraft?

The Overworld uses true darkness: with no light source it's pitch black. Carry torches. Fullbright resource packs are forbidden on Gilly SMP.

How does sleeping work in Minecraft multiplayer?

Sleeping doesn't skip the night instantly. Time speeds up while players sleep, and the more players are asleep, the faster the night passes. The action bar shows how many players are sleeping.

Do explosions destroy blocks in Minecraft?

On Gilly SMP, explosions drop all the blocks they destroy, so you keep the materials even when a blast goes off near your base.
