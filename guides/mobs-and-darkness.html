Mobs, phantoms and true darkness on Gilly SMP explained

Gilly SMP's survival rework changes where monsters spawn and how light works. These answers cover the open Overworld, caves, phantoms, sleeping and the late-game change after the Ender Dragon.

8 answersFull wiki →

Where monsters spawn

Why are there no creepers or spiders outside on Gilly SMP?

Creepers, spiders, endermen, witches and slimes do not spawn in the open Overworld on Gilly SMP. Zombies, skeletons and other undead still spawn at night, so carry a weapon and light. Caves and ruins are where the real danger is.

Are phantoms disabled on Gilly SMP?

Yes. Phantoms are disabled entirely on Gilly SMP, so you do not need to worry about them appearing when you sleep through nights.

When does the Overworld become safe on Gilly SMP?

After the Ender Dragon is defeated for the first time, evil is banished from the surface for everyone: nothing hostile spawns in the open any more. Caves and other dangerous places remain dangerous.

Do explosions destroy blocks on Gilly SMP?

Explosions drop every block they destroy on Gilly SMP, so an explosion is an inconvenience rather than a permanent loss. This applies along with the reduced surface spawning, which makes the open Overworld much calmer than vanilla.

Light, sleeping and difficulty

Why is it so dark on Gilly SMP?

The Overworld uses true darkness on Gilly SMP: without a light source it is pitch black. Carry torches when you explore, and note that fullbright resource packs are forbidden.

Does sleeping skip the night on Gilly SMP?

No. Sleeping does not skip the night instantly; instead time speeds up, and the more players who are sleeping, the faster it passes. The action bar shows how many players are asleep.

Are some mobs weaker on Gilly SMP?

Yes. On Easy difficulty, which is what Gilly SMP runs, some mobs such as creepers, skeletons and cave spiders are toned down. The difficulty never rises on its own. On Normal the rework could switch the world to Hard as players progress, but on Easy it stays Easy.

What difficulty does Gilly SMP run?

Gilly SMP runs on Easy, the difficulty the survival rework is balanced around for relaxed play. A short welcome message in chat when you log in shows the current mode, and the difficulty never increases by itself.
