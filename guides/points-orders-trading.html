Minecraft Server Economy: Points, Buy Orders and Player Trading Explained

Some survival servers run a player-driven economy instead of a shop, so items change hands between players. This guide explains points, buy orders, trades and referral rewards. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

8 answersFull wiki →

Points and paying players

How does a player-driven economy work on a Minecraft survival server?

On MatchaSMP the economy is entirely player-driven. Players sell items to each other through the order market or trades, and every price on the server is in points, the server currency.

How do I earn points on a Minecraft survival server?

New players start with 0 points. You earn them by selling items to other players through the order market, since points are not handed out by the server.

How do I pay another player in Minecraft?

Use /points pay <player> <amount>. Amounts are whole numbers of at least 1, and paying less than 1 is refused.

Buy orders

How do Minecraft buy orders work on a survival server?

Open the order market with /order and post a buy order with an item, an amount and a price per item of at least 1 point. The points are reserved, and other players deliver the items and get paid instantly, even while you're offline.

How many buy orders can I have at once in Minecraft?

On MatchaSMP you can have 5 active orders, with more allowed by rank: 7 on Bronze, 10 on Gold and 15 on Diamond. Orders expire after 14 days.

Do Minecraft buy orders need the exact item?

Yes. Items must match the order exactly, including enchantments, damage, custom names and contents. Items can be delivered from shulker boxes.

Trading and referrals

How do I trade items with another player in Minecraft?

Crouch and right-click the player, or use /trade <player>. You must be within 10 blocks, in the same world and not in creative. Both sides can offer items and points, and both must accept. XP can't be traded.

How do referral rewards work on a Minecraft server?

Run /referral to see your personal code and give it to a friend. When they join and type /refer <code>, they get 5 diamonds right away and you get 10 once they've played for 2 hours. You can refer up to 5 players, one per IP address.
