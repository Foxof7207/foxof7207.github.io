Gilly SMP alloys and stations: steel, Hepatizon, kilns and tools

The Gilly SMP survival rework adds metals, alloys and extra processing stations before the furnace. These answers explain which alloy goes on which metal, what each station does, and why raw iron cannot go in the Mud Kiln.

9 answersFull wiki →

Alloys and metals

How do alloys work on Gilly SMP?

Each metal has built-in properties: gold has Fortune and Looting, while iron and diamond can host alloys. Alloys are smithed onto a base metal rather than replacing it. Gilly SMP has about 1275 recipes in total, many of them for these alloys and their components.

Which alloys go on copper, iron and diamond?

Hepatizon and Shakudo are smithed onto copper, Steel onto iron, and Electrum and Adamant onto diamond. These alloys are a core part of progression, so each tier opens new tools and equipment.

Where do I find silver on Gilly SMP?

Silver is an inlay found in mountains and Abbeys. Those structures and biomes are the places to look when your progression calls for silver rather than a standard ore.

What are mattocks and dolabras on Gilly SMP?

Mattocks and Dolabras are two-in-one tools in the survival rework. They combine the functions of separate vanilla tools, which is useful when the rework slows progression and you want fewer tool switches.

Which Minecraft datapack adds Hepatizon, Steel and Electrum?

The Matcha Flavoured datapack adds four alloys: Hepatizon and Shakudo on copper, Steel on iron, and Electrum and Adamant on diamond. Gilly SMP runs it with server-side recipes, plus stations like the Mud Kiln and Blast Furnace, and you can look anything up in the Recipe Book.

Stations and smelting

What stations come before the furnace on Gilly SMP?

Progression starts with the campfire and Mud Kiln, then moves to the kiln and Oven, and after that the Blast Furnace, Smithing Table and anvil. Take your time at each tier; the rework is built around discovering the next station through advancements.

Why can't I smelt iron in the Mud Kiln on Gilly SMP?

The Mud Kiln only smelts copper and earthen blocks such as bricks, glass, smooth stone and terracotta. Raw iron and raw gold need a Blast Furnace, crafted from 4 Polished Deepslate, 2 Smooth Stone and a Kindling.

What does the Oven do on Gilly SMP?

The Oven is the rework's cooking block, and it only cooks food. It is not the block for metals: raw iron and gold go in the Blast Furnace, while copper and earthen materials go in the Mud Kiln.

What is a Kindling used for on Gilly SMP?

Kindling is a component in the recipe for a Blast Furnace, which is crafted from 4 Polished Deepslate, 2 Smooth Stone and a Kindling. Kindling recipes are part of the custom survival rework and appear in the Recipe Book.
