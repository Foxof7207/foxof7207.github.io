How to Make Steel, Hepatizon and Other Alloys in a Minecraft Survival Rework

This survival rework adds metal alloys, a chain of crafting stations and two-in-one tools. Progress comes in tiers, so it helps to know what each station is for and what it can smelt. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

8 answersFull wiki →

Alloys and metals

What is an alloy in Minecraft?

An alloy is a metal smithed onto a base metal. On MatchaSMP the alloys are Hepatizon and Shakudo on copper, Steel on iron, and Electrum and Adamant on diamond.

How do you make steel in Minecraft?

Steel is an alloy smithed onto iron. Alloys are smithed onto a base metal: Hepatizon and Shakudo on copper, Steel on iron, and Electrum and Adamant on diamond.

Which metals have special properties in this Minecraft survival rework?

Each metal has built-in properties: gold has Fortune and Looting, while iron and diamond host alloys. This is why alloys are the main way to progress past the early tiers.

Where do you find silver in Minecraft?

Silver is an inlay found in mountains and in Abbeys.

Stations and smelting

In what order do the crafting stations unlock in Minecraft?

New stations come before the furnace: the campfire and Mud Kiln first, then the Kiln and Oven, then the Blast Furnace, the Smithing Table and the Anvil.

What does the Mud Kiln smelt in Minecraft?

The Mud Kiln only smelts copper and earthen blocks, such as bricks, glass, smooth stone and terracotta.

Why can't I smelt iron in the Mud Kiln?

Raw iron and gold need a Blast Furnace, crafted from 4 Polished Deepslate, 2 Smooth Stone and a Kindling. The furnace block is the Oven, and it only cooks food.

Tools

What are mattocks and dolabras in Minecraft?

Mattocks and dolabras are two-in-one tools in this survival rework, so one item covers two jobs. They are part of the tier progression, so check the recipes when you reach that stage.
