Gilly SMP rules, PvP, griefing, bans and quality-of-life features

This guide covers the rules that keep Gilly SMP fair and the small quality-of-life changes layered on top of the survival rework. Run /mechanics in game to see everything that is active.

Fair play rules

Is Gilly SMP pay to win?

No. Gilly SMP is strictly non pay-to-win: the store sells only quality-of-life items and cosmetics, nothing that gives a power advantage. Ranks are quality-of-life perks too, granted by staff.

Is PvP allowed on Gilly SMP?

Yes, PvP is allowed when it is fair. Teleport-trapping and spawn-killing are not. Teleports have a 3-second warmup that is cancelled by moving or taking damage, so you cannot teleport out of a fight.

Is griefing or stealing allowed on Gilly SMP?

No. Griefing and stealing are forbidden: the first offence gets a warning, and a second offence gets a temporary ban. There is no land claiming, so respect other players' builds.

Are cheat mods allowed on Gilly SMP?

No. An anti-cheat runs on the server, and mods such as X-ray, freecam and Wurst are banned. If staff suspect cheating, they may ask you to run a verification mod. Client-side performance, minimap and shader mods are fine.

Why was I muted on Gilly SMP?

Spam is detected automatically. Repeating the same message, flooding, very long messages, excessive capitals and random characters can trigger a short mute, and it lasts longer if it keeps happening.

Staff, appeals and commands

How do I contact staff or appeal a ban on Gilly SMP?

Open a ticket on Discord, which is the fastest route, or email gilly-smp@outlook.com. Ban appeals and staff contact both go through those channels.

Why does Gilly SMP say my command is not available?

Only player commands are enabled on Gilly SMP. Anything else is blocked, hidden from tab completion and answers 'That command is not available'. Bedrock players have almost all available commands as buttons in /menu.

Which commands can I use on Gilly SMP?

Commands cover homes and teleporting (/spawn, /sethome, /tpa), chat (/msg, /ignore), economy and storage (/points, /order, /trade, /ec), tools (/ch, /afk, /mechanics), referrals and /menu for Bedrock. The complete list is in All commands.

Quality-of-life tweaks

How do I show my coordinates on Gilly SMP?

Use /ch toggle (also /coordshud) to show XYZ, facing direction and a 24-hour in-game clock in your action bar.

Can I lock chests or item frames on Gilly SMP?

Yes. Chests can be locked so only you and players you allow can open them. Crouch and right-click an item frame with shears to lock or unlock it, or with a brush to make it invisible.

How do I keep a chunk loaded on Gilly SMP?

Drop a Nether Star on a lodestone. The chunk stays loaded until the lodestone is broken. This is one of the quality-of-life tweaks active on the server.

Can I sit on stairs or merge an elytra with a chestplate?

Yes to both. Right-click a stair with a saddle, then right-click it again to sit; break the stair to get the saddle back. For an elytra, drop it together with a chestplate on an anvil, and drop the result on a grindstone to split them again while keeping enchantments.
