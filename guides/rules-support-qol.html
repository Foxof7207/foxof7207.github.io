Minecraft Server Rules, Anti-Cheat and Quality-of-Life Tweaks Explained

Good survival servers are defined by their rules as much as their features: what fights are fair, what counts as griefing and which tweaks make everyday play easier. These answers cover both. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

9 answersFull wiki →

Fair play

Is PvP allowed on a Minecraft survival server?

On MatchaSMP, PvP is allowed if it's fair. Tp-trapping and spawn-killing are not. Teleports have a 3-second warmup that moving or taking damage cancels, so you can't teleport out of a fight.

Are cheat mods allowed on a Minecraft server?

No. An anti-cheat runs on MatchaSMP, and mods like X-ray, freecam or Wurst are banned. Suspected players may be asked to run a verification mod.

What is the punishment for griefing on a Minecraft server?

On MatchaSMP, griefing and stealing are not allowed. The first offence gets a warning, and the second gets a temporary ban.

Can you claim land on a Minecraft survival server?

There is no land claiming on MatchaSMP. Respect other players' builds.

Quality of life

How do I lock a chest or hide an item frame in Minecraft?

On MatchaSMP, chests can be locked so only you and the players you allow can open them. Crouch and right-click an item frame with shears to lock or unlock it, or with a brush to make it invisible.

How do I keep a chunk loaded in Minecraft?

Drop a Nether Star on a lodestone. On MatchaSMP the chunk stays loaded until the lodestone is broken.

How do I merge an elytra with a chestplate in Minecraft?

Drop both on an anvil. Enchantments are kept. Drop the result on a grindstone to split them again.

Is it pay-to-win, and how do I appeal?

Is this Minecraft server pay-to-win?

No. MatchaSMP is non pay-to-win: the store only sells quality-of-life items and cosmetics.

How do I appeal a ban on a Minecraft server?

On MatchaSMP, open a ticket on Discord, the fastest route, or email the staff address listed on the wiki's support section.
