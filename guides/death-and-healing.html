Minecraft Hearts Explained: Death Penalties, Healing and Crystal Hearts

Health works differently on this survival rework: natural regeneration is off, hunger is gone, and death costs maximum hearts rather than items. These answers explain the rules in plain language. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

8 answersFull wiki →

Healing

Why doesn't my health regenerate in Minecraft?

On this survival rework, natural health regeneration is turned off. You heal with food effects, potions and special items instead, so bring food when you explore.

Does hunger matter in a Minecraft survival rework?

Hunger is effectively gone on MatchaSMP: you won't starve, and food is valued for its effects instead of filling a hunger bar.

Which items heal you in a Minecraft survival rework?

On MatchaSMP you heal with foods that have healing effects, potions and special items. Food is valued for its effects rather than for filling a bar.

How do I get hearts back in Minecraft after dying?

Crystal Hearts add a maximum heart back, up to a total of 30. Each death costs one maximum heart, so Crystal Hearts are how you recover lost ground.

What dying costs

What happens to my items when I die in Minecraft survival?

On MatchaSMP you keep your inventory and XP when you die. Each death removes 1 maximum heart instead, and thrown ender pearls don't vanish.

What is the minimum number of hearts in Minecraft on a server?

On MatchaSMP, which runs on Easy, you never drop below 10 hearts from deaths.

What is a maximum heart in Minecraft?

A maximum heart is the most hearts you can reach. On MatchaSMP each death removes one, and Crystal Hearts add one back, up to a total of 30.

Do you lose XP when you die in Minecraft?

No. On MatchaSMP you keep your XP on death, along with your inventory.
