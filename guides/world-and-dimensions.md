# Minecraft World Guide: Difficulty, Nether, End, Seasons and World Resets

A survival server's world rules decide how the game feels: difficulty, what generates, when new dimensions open and when the world resets. These answers cover those rules in plain language. MatchaSMP is the worked example.

## Difficulty and terrain

### What difficulty does a Minecraft survival server run on?

MatchaSMP runs on **Easy**, the mode the survival rework is balanced around. Some mobs such as creepers, skeletons and cave spiders are toned down, and the difficulty never rises on its own.

### What does the terrain look like on this Minecraft survival server?

MatchaSMP uses vanilla terrain and biomes, with extra ores, trees and structures such as abbeys and beta villages. Everything uses vanilla blocks, so no mods are needed.

### Are there villages in this Minecraft survival world?

The survival rework expects you to build your own village on MatchaSMP. Advancements explain how to bring villagers to it.

### Is there a world border on this Minecraft server?

No world border is set on MatchaSMP yet. If one is added, its size will be listed on the wiki.

## Nether, End and portals

### Can I go to the Nether on a Minecraft survival server?

Not yet. On MatchaSMP both the Nether and the End are closed for now and will open in stages. The Nether opens after the next major update.

### How do I line up Nether portals on a Minecraft server?

Once the Nether opens on MatchaSMP, `/portal` tells you where to build a portal in the other dimension so the two line up. Nether portals can be any size or shape.

### Does the Minecraft world reset on a survival server?

Yes. On MatchaSMP the world resets at the end of every season. You can ask for a copy of the world on Discord one week after the season ends.

## Getting around

### Is there a random teleport command on a Minecraft server?

There is no `/rtp` command on MatchaSMP. Instead, an RTP cannon at spawn fires you 500 to 5000 blocks away onto safe ground, with a slow fall and a 30-second cooldown.
