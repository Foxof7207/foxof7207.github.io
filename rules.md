# Gilly-SMP Server Rules

These rules apply to the Minecraft server, Discord, voice chat, claims, shops, teams, events, reports, and all official Gilly-SMP spaces.

By playing on Gilly-SMP, you agree to follow these rules and respect staff decisions.


## 1. Respect Other Players

Treat everyone with respect.

Not allowed:

* Harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted insults
* Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or any discriminatory language
* Slurs, hate speech, or extremist content
* Excessive toxicity, drama-baiting, or personal attacks
* Ping spam, chat spam, or intentionally annoying players or staff
* Impersonating staff or another player
* Encouraging others to break rules

Keep arguments out of public chat. If there is a serious issue, report it to staff.


## 2. No Cheating, Hacked Clients, or Exploits

Play fairly. Anything that gives an unfair advantage is not allowed.

Strictly forbidden:

* X-ray, cave finder, ore finder, or texture packs made to reveal hidden blocks
* Hacked clients, including Meteor Client
* Kill aura, reach, fly, speed, anti-knockback, auto-totem, or similar cheats
* Auto-clickers, macros, or bots used for unfair gameplay
* Freecam abuse to locate bases, caves, spawners, ores, hidden areas, or private builds
* Duping, economy exploits, claim bypasses, or bug abuse
* Using alternate accounts to bypass punishments or gain advantages

Usually allowed:

* Performance mods such as Sodium, Lithium, Iris, or OptiFine
* Shaders
* Cosmetic mods
* Simple Voice Chat
* Minimap mods, as long as cave mode, entity radar, and player radar are disabled

Ask staff first if you are unsure whether a mod is allowed.


## 3. No Griefing

Do not damage, destroy, modify, or ruin anything that does not belong to you.

This includes:

* Breaking or changing another player’s build
* Destroying farms, redstone, villagers, spawners, portals, roads, or public infrastructure
* Lava, water, fire, TNT, creeper, or wither griefing
* Placing blocks to annoy, trap, or block players
* Damaging spawn, shops, community builds, paths, Nether hubs, or public farms
* Building offensive, inappropriate, or intentionally disruptive structures near someone else’s base
* Sabotaging team bases, public projects, or event areas

Accidents happen. Report accidental damage to staff immediately.


## 4. No Stealing

Do not take anything that is not yours.

This includes:

* Items from chests, barrels, shulkers, furnaces, hoppers, item frames, armor stands, or shops
* Drops from another player’s farm without permission
* Villagers, mobs, pets, armor, tools, blocks, or currency
* Death items, unless the owner gives permission
* Items from abandoned-looking bases unless staff confirms they are free to loot
* Items from team storage unless your team allows it

Do not retaliate if someone steals from you. Report it to staff so it can be investigated and restored.


## 5. Claim Rules

Claims are for protecting active builds, not blocking other players.

Rules:

* Only claim what you actively use
* Do not overclaim large areas without a real build plan
* Do not claim around, inside, or directly beside another player’s base without permission
* Do not intentionally claim natural structures such as Nether Fortresses, Trial Chambers, Ancient Cities, Strongholds, End Cities, Ocean Monuments, or Woodland Mansions
* Do not claim public roads, spawn areas, Nether hubs, portals, community builds, or public farms without staff approval
* Do not use inappropriate claim names
* Do not use claims to trap, block, scam, or grief players
* Do not use claims to block access to another player’s base, portal, road, or project

Staff may resize, remove, or transfer claims if they are abusive, inactive, or blocking normal gameplay.


## 6. PvP Rules

PvP must be fair and consensual unless a server event clearly says otherwise.

Not allowed:

* Spawn killing
* Killing AFK players
* Repeatedly targeting the same player
* Combat logging
* Teleport trapping or teleport-kill baiting
* Portal trapping
* Bed, claim, or home trapping
* Killing players during trades unless both players agreed to PvP
* Using bugs, claims, or server mechanics to force unfair fights
* Starting fights only to harass or annoy players

If PvP is abused, staff may disable your PvP access or apply additional punishments.


## 7. Bases, Land, and Player Property

Respect other players’ space.

Rules:

* Do not enter private bases without permission if the owner has asked people to stay out
* Do not build too close to another player’s base without asking
* Do not use someone else’s farms, villagers, portals, storage, or redstone without permission
* Do not move into abandoned bases unless staff confirms they are abandoned
* Do not modify public builds unless you have permission or are clearly helping maintain them
* Do not create lag machines or intentionally harmful redstone systems

When in doubt, ask first.


## 8. Team Rules

Teams are part of the SMP, but team rivalry must stay within the rules.

Rules:

* Private team Discord channels are only visible to members of the matching team and staff
* Do not leak private team messages, coordinates, plans, screenshots, strategies, or storage information without permission
* Do not use alternate accounts or outside methods to access another team’s private information
* Do not pressure members of other teams to leak private information
* Do not use team channels to organize griefing, stealing, harassment, cheating, exploiting, or rule-breaking
* Do not sabotage another team through griefing, theft, spying, or harassment
* Do not use team rivalry as an excuse to break normal server rules

Friendly rivalry is allowed. Spying, leaking, harassment, and sabotage are not.


## 9. Economy, Shops, and Trading

Trade honestly.

Not allowed:

* Scamming
* Fake shops
* Fake bids
* Misleading prices
* Refusing to pay after agreeing to a trade
* Taking from shops without paying
* Selling stolen items
* Abusing bugs to gain money, items, or shop stock
* Using alternate accounts to manipulate the economy
* Using Discord trades or auctions to avoid server rules

Shop owners must keep their shops clear, functional, and not intentionally misleading.


## 10. Keep Chat, Names, and Builds Clean

Keep the server appropriate for the community.

Not allowed:

* NSFW, explicit, sexual, graphic, or disturbing content
* Inappropriate skins, usernames, nicknames, item names, claim names, signs, books, maps, banners, or builds
* Advertising other Minecraft servers or server IPs
* Spam, flooding chat, or excessive caps
* Malicious links, unsafe downloads, scams, or phishing
* Political or religious arguments that become disruptive
* Hate symbols, slurs, extremist content, or offensive jokes aimed at protected groups

Staff may remove inappropriate content without warning.


## 11. Voice Chat Rules

Voice chat must follow the same standards as text chat.

Rules:

* Use Discord or Simple Voice Chat responsibly
* Avoid excessive background noise
* Use push-to-talk when possible
* Do not scream, ear-rape, spam sounds, or play loud music
* Do not harass, threaten, or insult players in voice chat
* Do not record players without permission unless the recording is needed as evidence for a serious report
* Do not join voice channels only to annoy people

Voice chat abuse may result in a mute, kick, or further punishment.


## 12. Events and Community Areas

Server events and community builds may have extra rules.

Rules:

* Follow event instructions from staff
* Do not sabotage events
* Do not steal event items or rewards
* Do not grief arenas, minigames, roads, hubs, public farms, or spawn builds
* Do not abuse event mechanics for personal gain
* Do not use alternate accounts to enter giveaways, claim rewards, or gain advantages

Event-specific rules override normal gameplay rules during that event.


## 13. Reporting Problems

Report issues to staff instead of handling them yourself.

Use:

* `/report`
* `/bugreport`
* Discord support channels
* Helpers or staff members

Report:

* Theft
* Griefing
* Cheating
* Spawn killing
* Bugs or exploits
* Inappropriate behavior
* Claim abuse
* Shop scams
* Team leaks or private channel abuse

When reporting, include:

* Player name
* What happened
* Approximate time and date
* Screenshots, clips, or other evidence
* Coordinates if relevant
* Any witnesses

Do not fake reports. False reports or edited evidence may be punished.

## 14. Staff Decisions and Enforcement

Staff may take action when rules are broken or when behavior harms the server.

Possible punishments:

* Warning
* Mute
* Kick
* Jail
* Item removal
* Economy penalty
* Claim removal
* PvP disable
* Account lock
* Temporary ban
* Permanent ban

Punishments may depend on:

* Severity
* Intent
* Previous history
* Cooperation with staff
* Damage caused to players, builds, teams, economy, or the server
* Whether the player reported the issue honestly

Staff may act on behavior not listed here if it damages the server, community, economy, teams, staff, or player experience.


## 15. Rule Updates

Rules may be changed at any time. Important updates will be announced on the official Discord.
