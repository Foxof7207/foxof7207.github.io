# 🪵 Driftwood

![Driftwood Logo](/logos/driftwood_logo.png)

Welcome to the official recruitment page for the **Driftwood**! We are a new lifesteal guild ready to dominate oceans, conquer islands and forests, and defend our land at all costs.

## 📖 About Us

Driftwood is brand new, so this is the start of our story. We’re building from nothing and looking for players who want to grow into a powerful force together. Our vibe is competitive, loyal, and focused on smart teamwork, raids, and territory control.

We believe in:
- **Loyalty**: We stick together in fights and always back up guild members.
- **Strength**: We train, raid, and improve so we can win in lifesteal combat.
- **Expansion**: We actively conquer islands, forests, and key areas to grow our power.

## ⚔️ What We Offer

- **Founding Member Status**: Join early and help shape ranks, strategy, and the guild’s future.
- **Raid and PvP Teamplay**: Fight with coordinated teammates instead of surviving alone.
- **Territory and Resource Growth**: Be part of conquering and developing islands, forests, and new bases.

## 📝 How to Join

We want active, loyal players who enjoy lifesteal PvP, raiding, and building a strong guild from the ground up.

To join the Driftwood:
1. Join the Gilly-SMP server.
2. Message **PinneaplePenguin** in server chat and ask to join.
3. We’ll do a quick vibe check and invite active players!

*Take hearts. Claim land. Rule the wild.*
