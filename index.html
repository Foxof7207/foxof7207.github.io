What is Gilly SMP?

A Minecraft survival SMP for players who like to take their time.

We run a custom survival rework: new alloys, foods, structures and villager trades, no natural regeneration, and a surface with no creepers or spiders in the open. You won't need mob farms or XP farms to keep up.

Why Play Gilly SMP?

Survival that rewards patience instead of farms.

Reworked progression

Copper, iron and diamond each lead to their own alloys. The advancements guide you, one tier at a time.

Forgiving deaths

You keep your inventory. Each death costs a heart, and you never drop below ten.

A world worth exploring

Abbeys to raid, around a hundred fish to catch and a surface that gets safer as the server progresses.

Homes and teleports

Homes, /tpa, an RTP cannon, elevators and an ender chest you can open anywhere.

Made for Bedrock too

Bedrock and console players get a touch menu with /menu instead of typing commands.

FAQ

Short answers to the questions we hear most. The full answers, with every command and rule, are in the wiki FAQ.

Do I need mods?

No. The Gilly SMP resource pack is sent automatically when you join Java. Voice chat is an optional mod (Simple Voice Chat).

Is it hard?

The server runs on Easy. Early game is slower than vanilla, but deaths are forgiving and the advancements tell you what to do next.

Is there a whitelist?

No. Anyone with a legitimate Minecraft account can join.

Does the world reset?

Yes, every season. You can ask for a copy of the old world on Discord one week after the season ends.

Can I keep my progress if I switch platforms?

Yes. Link your Java and Bedrock accounts with /linkaccount and they share one profile.
