Getting started

What kind of server is Gilly SMP?

A slow-paced, non pay-to-win survival SMP (Easy difficulty, up to 20 players, online mode, no whitelist) with a custom survival rework. Progression is reworked and slower so casual players can keep up without mob farms or XP farms. Java, Bedrock and console players share one world.

How do I join?

Use the How to Join cards. Java: mc.gillyb.net (Minecraft Java 26.3, required). Bedrock: mc.gillyb.net, port 19132 (on Windows, Android, iPhone and iPad, the Add to Minecraft button on the Bedrock card opens the game with the server already added). Console: add GillySMP as a friend and join from the Friends tab (your console's online subscription is required by the console maker).

Do I need a paid Minecraft account?

Yes. The server runs in online mode, so cracked or offline launchers can't join.

Do I need a resource pack?

On Java, yes: the server adds hundreds of custom items and they need the Gilly SMP resource pack. The server sends it automatically when you join: just accept the prompt, there is nothing to download. Without it, items look vanilla and some have raw text names. Bedrock can't load Java packs, so Bedrock players always see vanilla textures and some raw keys; everything still works.

Do I need any mods?

No. Client-side mods (performance, minimaps, shaders, voice chat) are fine. Cheat mods (X-ray, freecam, hacked clients) are not, and the server runs anti-cheat. The installation of a verification mod can be requested by the staff in the event of suspicions.

Why does my Bedrock name start with an underscore?

Bedrock names get a _ prefix (and spaces become underscores) so they never clash with Java names. The prefix is the same on the proxy and on the server, so you keep one name everywhere.

I play on Java and Bedrock. Can I keep my progress?

Yes. Run /linkaccount on one account and follow the instructions to link it to the other. /unlinkaccount undoes it. Once linked, the two accounts share one player profile.

What should I do first?

Open your advancements (L). The survival rework uses them as a step-by-step guide, and the recipe book unlocks recipes when you need them. Early game is slower than vanilla and natural health regeneration is off. Set a home once you have a base (/sethome base), and /spawn takes you back to spawn at any time.

What are the first commands I'll want?

/menu (Bedrock only), /spawn, /sethome base, /homes, /points me and /mechanics (lists every tweak active on the server). The full list is in All commands.

Survival rework

The custom rework of survival on Gilly SMP: slow, casual play with no mob farms, no XP farms and no villager trading halls. We ask players to discover it through the advancements (press L), so this section stays spoiler-light.

What is the survival rework?

A set of changes built for Minecraft 26.3. It adds about 1275 recipes, 880 advancements, 290 villager trades, 31 new and 43 overridden enchantments, 13 structures, 7 music discs, 10 banner patterns, 3 trim materials and about 50 foods. It replaces many vanilla advancements and recipes with its own.

Why can't I heal?

Natural health regeneration is off. You heal with food effects, potions and special items. Hunger is effectively gone: you won't starve, and food is about its effects instead of filling a bar.

What happens when I die?

You keep your inventory and XP, but lose 1 maximum heart. On Easy (this server) you never drop below 10 hearts. Crystal Hearts add a maximum heart back, up to 30. Thrown ender pearls don't vanish on death.

Why are there so few monsters outside?

Creepers, spiders, endermen, witches and slimes don't spawn in the open Overworld. Zombies, skeletons and other undead still do at night, so bring a weapon and light. Caves and ruins are the real danger. Phantoms are disabled, and explosions drop all the blocks they destroy.

Spoiler: late game

Once the Ender Dragon is defeated for the first time, evil is banished from the surface for everyone: nothing hostile spawns in the open any more.

Why is it so dark?

The Overworld uses true darkness: without a light source it's pitch black. Carry torches. Beware that fullbright ressource packs are forbidden !

How does sleeping work?

Night doesn't skip instantly. Time speeds up while players sleep, and the more players sleep, the faster it goes. The action bar shows how many players are sleeping.

How does progression work?

Each metal has built-in properties (gold has Fortune and Looting, iron and diamond host alloys). Alloys are smithed onto a base metal: Hepatizon and Shakudo on copper, Steel on iron, Electrum and Adamant on diamond. Silver is an inlay found in mountains and Abbeys. New stations come before the furnace: campfire and mud kiln, then kiln and oven, then the blast furnace, the smithing table and the anvil. Mattocks and Dolabras are two-in-one tools. Take your time at each tier.

I play on Bedrock. How do I follow the advancements?

Bedrock can't open the advancements menu, so we made the Advancement Guide: every step in order, what completes it, its rewards and the Angler's Almanac fish list. You can tick steps off as you go.

Where can I see all the recipes?

In the Recipe Book: every recipe of the survival rework, searchable, including the Mud Kiln, Oven, Blast Furnace, Kindling and Smithing Table recipes that Bedrock's in-game recipe book can't show. Tap an item to see how to make it and what it's used in. It contains spoilers, so use it when you're stuck.

I'm stuck at the start.

Check the advancements and the recipes you can't craft yet. Start with a campfire, a wooden hoe and dried grass, then light kindling with tinder or a flint firestarter.

Where are the villages?

Villages are meant to be built, not found. The advancements explain how to bring villagers home.

Can I break spawners?

Yes, and you should: they drop rare materials and Divine Fragments. Don't use spawn eggs on them, it destroys nearby spawners and hurts you.

Can I go to the Nether or the End?

Not yet. Both are closed and will open in stages. The Nether opens after the next major update, so it generates with the newest version of the rework.

What about fishing, food and enchanting?

The Angler's Almanac adds around 100 fish depending on biome and water temperature, plus junk and treasure; sell your catch to a Fisherman villager. About 50 new foods and meals exist, and 12 ingredients carry hidden effects revealed by cooking. Enchanting is reworked, with new enchantments, and anvil repairs never become "Too Expensive!". There are also 7 new music discs, 10 pride banner patterns and new trim materials.

Spoiler: the early-game path

1. Make a campfire and a wooden hoe. Harvest grass with the hoe and dry it on the campfire.
2. Light kindling with tinder or a flint firestarter.
3. Build a Mud Kiln from packed mud and mudbricks, and smelt copper in it.
4. Make bricks and a ceramic bowl, then an Oven from bricks and iron to cook proper meals.
5. Iron → Carbon-Rich Iron (iron + coal block) → blast it into Steel.
6. A steel Anvil repairs equipment forever; a Smithing Table lets you upgrade with alloys.

Spoiler: treasures and late game

Gems ("Cantos"): Opal (defeat the Sea God), Ruby (pillage the remnants of Pandaemonium), Amber (exhume the Pilgrim's Trails), Topaz (raid Tartarus). Divine Fragments: only from breaking spawners or defeating powerful bosses. Crystal Hearts: eating one gives an extra maximum heart (it hurts). Lapis Nazar: wards off the dead when held, and can be attached to some equipment. Obols: currency-like treasure. Clay Fetishes and statues: found at abandoned sites. Beacon Kindling: signals a Wandering Trader, who arrives about 10 minutes later and stays 5. Nether: Estus, Benzene, Blessings, Hell-Bound Books and Warding Stones. End: Hepatizon and Shakudo Elytra. Bedrock Buster: a special explosive that breaks bedrock. The Wither can only be summoned on the Overworld surface.

World & progression

What is the world like?

Vanilla terrain and biomes, with extra ores, trees and structures such as abbeys and beta villages. Everything uses vanilla blocks, so no mods are needed.

What difficulty does the server run?

Easy, the mode the survival rework is balanced around for relaxed play. Some mobs (creepers, skeletons, cave spiders) are toned down, and the difficulty never rises on its own. On Normal the rework can switch the world to Hard as players progress; on Easy it stays Easy. When you log in, chat shows a short welcome message and the current mode.

What are the death rules?

You keep all your items and XP when you die. Each death removes 1 maximum heart; on Easy you never go below 10 hearts. Crystal Hearts add a maximum heart, up to 30. Natural regeneration is off, so heal with food effects, potions and special items. Thrown ender pearls don't vanish when you die.

Are there any mobs on the surface?

Some. Undead (zombies, skeletons...) still spawn at night, but creepers, spiders and other non-undead monsters don't spawn in the open. Caves and ruins are where the real danger is. Phantoms are disabled, and explosions drop all the blocks they destroy.

How does sleeping work?

Sleeping doesn't skip the night instantly. Instead, time speeds up, and the more players are asleep, the faster the night passes. The action bar shows how many players are sleeping.

How dark does it get?

The Overworld uses true darkness: with no light source, it's pitch black. Carry torches.

Are the Nether and the End open?

No, both portals are closed for now and will be opened in stages so everyone can enjoy the early game first. The Nether opens after the next major update. When it does: /portal tells you where to build a portal in the other dimension so the two line up, and Nether portals can be any size or shape.

Is there a world border?

There's no border set on the world yet. If one is added, the size will be listed here.

What happens to my items if I lose a rank?

They stay in your ender chest. You can only reach the rows your current rank allows.

How do I see my rank?

Your rank shows in front of your name in chat and in the tab list. Bedrock players can see their rank and every rank's limits in /menu → My rank.

How do I get a rank?

Ranks are granted by staff. Have a look at the store and ask in Discord — note that ranks are a quality-of-life perk, not a power advantage.

Homes & teleporting

All teleports have a 3-second warmup: stand still and don't take damage, or the teleport is cancelled. This stops anyone using teleports to escape a fight.

How do I go to spawn?

/spawn. Staff set the spawn point in game, so it's the same for everyone.

How do I set a home?

/sethome <name> where you stand. You get 2 homes, more with a rank. Names can use letters, numbers, - and _, up to 16 characters. Using an existing name moves that home.

How do I go home?

/home <name>, or just /home if you have one. /homes lists them; click one to go there.

How do I delete or edit a home?

/delhome <name> deletes it. /edithome <name> renames, moves, describes it or makes it public or private.

How do public homes work?

Make a home public with /edithome and choose the privacy option. Others list public homes with /phomes and visit with /phome <owner>+<home>, for example /phome Steve+shop. Note the + between the owner and the home name.

How do I teleport to another player?

/tpa <player> asks to go to them, /tpahere <player> asks them to come to you. They answer with /tpaccept or /tpdecline (or the chat buttons, Java only) within 60 seconds. /tpignore <player> blocks requests from someone; run it again to undo.

Is there a random teleport?

There's no /rtp command. Instead there's an RTP cannon: step into it and it fires you 500 to 5000 blocks away onto safe ground, with a slow fall. 30-second cooldown. The cannon is placed at spawn; ask in Discord if you can't find it.

How do elevators work?

Drop an ender pearl on a wool block to turn it into an elevator. Jump on it to go to the next elevator of the same colour above, crouch to go down.

Can I do all this on Bedrock?

Yes. /menu has Homes (a dropdown of your homes, plus set and delete) and Teleport requests (a dropdown of online players).

Points, orders & trading

What are points?

The server currency, used for orders and trades. /points me (or /p me) shows your balance, /points look <player> someone else's, /points lead the leaderboard. Every "money" price on the server is in points.

How do I get points?

New players start with 0 points. You earn them by selling items to other players through the order market or in trades — the server doesn't hand them out yet, so the economy is entirely player-driven.

How do I pay someone?

/points pay <player> <amount>. Whole numbers, at least 1. Paying less than 1 is refused.

How does the order market work?

/order (or /orders) opens it. Post a buy order (item, amount, price per item, at least 1 point); the points are reserved, other players deliver the items and get paid instantly, even while you're offline, and you collect the items later from Your orders. You can have 5 active orders, more with a rank. Orders expire after 14 days, new ones are announced in chat, and items can be delivered from shulker boxes. Items must match the order exactly — enchantments, damage, custom names and contents included.

How do I trade with a player?

Crouch and right-click them, or /trade <player>. You must be within 10 blocks, in the same world, and not in creative. There's a 20-second cooldown between trade requests. Both sides can offer items and points, and both must accept. XP can't be traded: the survival rework manages XP itself. Bedrock players can pick a player from /menu → Trade.

Ender chest

How big is my ender chest?

3 rows (27 slots), up to 6 rows (54 slots) with a rank. Open it anywhere with /ec. Bedrock, command blocks and barriers can't be stored. It's backed up automatically every night.

Can I share it?

/ec invite <player> gives access, /ec uninvite <player> removes it, /ec invitelist shows who has it. Invited players open it with /ecshare <owner>.

Can I rename it?

/ec rename <name>, or /ec rename reset.

What if something goes wrong?

Ask a staff member on Discord. Ender chests are backed up every night, so items can usually be restored.

Chat, voice & AFK

Who sees my messages?

Players within 100 blocks of you, in the same world. Start a message with ! to talk to everyone. Your rank shows in front of your name, for example [Gold] <Steve> hello.

How do I send a private message?

/msg <player> <message> (also /w, /tell, /m, /whisper), reply with /r. /ignore <player> hides someone, /unignore undoes it. /me sends an emote: /me waves shows * Steve waves. Bedrock players can pick a player from /menu → Private message.

Can I use colours?

& colour codes work in chat, on signs and in anvil names (up to 32 characters). A message starting with > is green (the > stays), one starting with * is a purple-to-blue gradient.

Is spam blocked?

Yes. Repeating the same message, flooding, very long messages, excessive capitals and random characters are detected automatically. Spammers are muted for a short time, longer if they keep doing it.

How does voice chat work?

Java players can install the Simple Voice Chat mod (Fabric, NeoForge, Forge or Quilt) matching Minecraft 26.3, join and press V to set it up. You hear players within 48 blocks (24 when whispering) and can make groups with G. Push-to-talk is on Caps Lock by default; switch to voice activation in the settings. Recording is disabled. Bedrock and console can't use it, so it's never required.

What happens when I'm AFK?

After 5 minutes without moving you're marked [AFK] and your name turns grey (/afk toggles it). After 10 more minutes you're disconnected, so the server stays fast for everyone. Just rejoin — and if the main server restarts while you're away, you'll land in the AFK zone and can come back with /server smp.

Bedrock menu

Is there a menu for Bedrock and console?

Yes. Type /menu to open a touch-friendly menu with buttons for the most common commands: Spawn, Homes, Teleport requests, Ender Chest, Orders, Trade, Private message, Points, Referrals, My rank and Tools. Whenever you need to pick a player, you get a list of everyone online.

Why is a button grey?

Buttons that need another player are greyed out with "nobody else is online" when you're alone. "Go to a home" is greyed out until you set one.

Clickable chat messages don't work on Bedrock.

That's a Bedrock limitation. Use /menu to accept or decline teleport requests and to pick homes. Java players don't need the menu; they use the commands directly.

Where do I see my rank and these limits?

In /menu → My rank, which lists your rank and the perks of every rank.

Quality-of-life features

Small tweaks that make survival nicer, added to the server. Most of them work without any command. Tweaks that bypassed the survival rework's progression (XP bottles, XP mending, unlocking every recipe, ore-block smelting and extra recipes for gated items) are turned off: when something here and the rework disagree, the rework's rules win. Run /mechanics in game to list everything that's active.

How do I show my coordinates?

/ch toggle (also /coordshud) shows XYZ, facing direction and a 24-hour in-game clock in your action bar.

How do I rename a name tag without an anvil?

Crouch and right-click with it.

How do I merge an elytra with a chestplate?

Drop both on an anvil. Drop the result on a grindstone to split them. Enchantments are kept.

How do I sit on stairs?

Right-click a stair with a saddle, then right-click it to sit. Break the stair to get the saddle back.

How do I keep a chunk loaded?

Drop a Nether Star on a lodestone. The chunk stays loaded until the lodestone is broken.

Can I be warned before a tool breaks?

Yes, when a tool or armour piece gets low. Customise it with /duraping config.

How do I lock or hide item frames?

Crouch and right-click with shears to lock or unlock it, with a brush to make it invisible.

What can cauldrons do?

Harden concrete powder, turn dirt into mud, fill and empty potions, tip arrows, and swap slime balls and magma cream (lava or water cauldron). Slime-block and honey-block cauldrons age or wax copper in bulk.

What's new in the stonecutter?

Mini blocks, wood items from planks and logs, painting variants, and engraving blank music discs (crafted from 8 iron ingots and a diamond).

Can I give my pet to someone?

Hold the lead attached to your tamed pet and right-click the other player.

Can I grow or shrink mobs?

Right-click with a Golden Dandelion: adults become babies and babies grow up. Works on villagers and zombies too.

What else is there for building?

Unwax signs by crouching and right-clicking a waxed sign with an axe, re-dye any dyed block to another colour (except concrete), and lectern books go back to page 1 when closed.

Other small tweaks?

Bone meal works on small flowers, sugar cane, cactus and leaf litter. Seeds on dirt make grass. An axe removes slime from sticky pistons; a slime ball makes a piston sticky again. Leaves decay fast. Husks drop sand, more mobs drop heads, a quarter of creepers explode into harmless confetti, and infested villagers turn into zombie villagers after a few minutes.

Are there extra recipes?

Yes: stairs, slabs and carpets back to blocks, double slabs from one block, unpacking wool/quartz/nether wart blocks/ice, more output for stairs, wooden trapdoors, bark and sherds, and craftable blackstone, gilded blackstone, calcite, tuff, gravel, coral blocks, small dripleaf, spore blossoms and copper golem statues. Blackstone replaces cobblestone in every cobblestone recipe, and sandstone + red dye makes red sandstone. The survival rework has its own recipes for bricks, packed mud, metal trapdoors, powered rails, chainmail, leather, black dye and glass: its advancements unlock them as you progress.

Can I start a thunderstorm?

Throw a Channeling trident at the sky (it costs 150 durability).

Are there graves?

No. The survival rework keeps your inventory on death, so you don't need one.

What does the chat announce?

Wandering traders that appear nearby (including the ones summoned by Beacon Kindling) and villager deaths, with the location.

Referrals

How do I invite a friend?

Run /referral to see your personal code, then give it to your friend. When they join, they type /refer <code>. Your friend gets 5 diamonds immediately, and you get 10 diamonds once they've played for 2 hours. Up to 5 referrals per player, one per IP address. /referraltop shows the leaderboard.

What are the rules?

A new player must use a code within their first 24 hours of playtime. You can refer up to 5 players, and only one referral counts per IP address, so alt accounts don't count. Playtime is taken from the server's analytics.

Is a free diamond a problem for progression?

It skips part of the way to diamonds on purpose, and staff know about it. If the reward changes to something that doesn't touch progression (points or cosmetics), this page will say so.

Rules

Is Gilly SMP pay-to-win?

No. The store only sells quality-of-life items and cosmetics.

Are cheats allowed?

No. An anti-cheat runs, and mods like X-ray, freecam or Wurst are banned. Suspected players may be asked to run a verification mod.

Is PvP allowed?

Yes, if it's fair. No tp-trapping or spawn-killing. Teleports have a 3-second warmup that moving or taking damage cancels, so you can't teleport out of a fight.

Are griefing and stealing allowed?

No. First offence: a warning. Second: a temporary ban.

Can I claim land?

No, there's no land claiming. Respect other players' builds.

Anything else?

Spam is detected and muted automatically, and the console and command blocks aren't available to players — only the commands in All commands.

Server info

Where is the server hosted?

In France. Players in the US get around 100 ms.

Is there a whitelist?

No. Anyone with a legitimate account can join.

Does the world reset?

Yes, at the end of every season. You can ask for a copy of the world on Discord one week after the season ends.

What is the world like?

Vanilla terrain and biomes, plus extra ores, trees and structures, all with vanilla blocks.

What are the server's limits?

Up to 20 players, Easy difficulty, online mode on (so a legitimate account is required) and no whitelist. The world resets at the end of each season.

What's the AFK zone?

If the server restarts or you're idle for 20 minutes, you're moved to a small waiting area instead of being disconnected. You get a chat message and a boss bar there, and /server smp brings you back once the server is up.

FAQ

The questions players ask most. For anything else, ask in Discord.

My items have raw text names and look like vanilla items.

You didn't accept the server's resource pack. Rejoin and accept it, or check Options → Resource Packs. See Getting started. On Bedrock this is expected and can't be fixed.

I can't heal!

Natural regeneration is off. Eat foods with healing effects, drink potions, or use special items. See Survival rework.

I keep losing hearts.

Every death costs 1 maximum heart, down to a minimum of 10 hearts. Crystal Hearts give hearts back. See World & progression.

I don't know what to do next.

Open your advancements (L). The advancements tell you the next step. On Bedrock, where that menu doesn't exist, use the Advancement Guide.

Why can't I smelt iron in the Kiln?

The Mud Kiln only smelts copper and earthen blocks (bricks, glass, smooth stone, terracotta). Raw iron and gold need a Blast Furnace, crafted from 4 Polished Deepslate, 2 Smooth Stone and a Kindling. The furnace block is the Oven and only cooks food. See the Blast Furnace recipes.

Where are the villages?

The survival rework expects you to build your own village. Advancements explain how to bring villagers to it.

Why are there so few monsters outside?

Creepers, spiders and other non-undead monsters don't spawn in the open. Zombies and skeletons still do at night.

"That command is not available"

Only player commands are enabled. See All commands.

How do I teleport to a friend?

/tpa <name>, then they type /tpaccept. See Homes & teleporting.

My chat messages aren't seen by everyone.

Chat only reaches players within 100 blocks. Start your message with ! to talk to everyone. See Chat, voice & AFK.

I got disconnected for being AFK.

Players AFK for 15 minutes are disconnected to keep the server fast. Just rejoin.

I'm on Bedrock and can't find a recipe.

Bedrock's recipe book only shows crafting-table recipes. Use the Recipe Book for everything else (Mud Kiln, Oven, Blast Furnace, Kindling, Smithing Table). It also tells you which vanilla item each custom item looks like on Bedrock.

I'm on Bedrock and typing commands is annoying.

Use /menu. See Bedrock menu.

How do I get a bigger ender chest or more homes?

With a rank.

Can I use mods?

Client-side mods (performance, minimap, shaders, voice chat) are fine. Cheating (X-ray, freecam, hacked clients) is not allowed, and the server runs anti-cheat.

Can I go to the Nether?

Not yet. See World & progression.
