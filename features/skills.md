# Skills & Enchants

Unlock powerful abilities, earn permanent stat bonuses, and enchant your gear through skill progression.

---

## Skills

Each skill has its own level and XP pool. Train by using the relevant tools and actions, and earn better bonuses as you progress.

### Core Mechanics

- **Leveling**: Gain XP for Farming, Foraging, Mining, Fishing, Excavation, Combat, and more.
- **Stats**: Boost **Strength**, **Toughness**, **Health**, **Luck**, **Speed**, **Mana**, and **Wisdom** as you level.
- **Perks**: Higher skill levels unlock stronger passive effects and improve your character’s performance.
- **Skill Specialization**: Use the correct actions for each skill to earn max XP and unlock rewards faster.

### Skill Overview

| Skill | Train By | Benefits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Farming** | Harvest crops, shear sheep, breed animals | Health, regeneration, crop yield |
| **Foraging** | Chop wood, gather plants, break leaves | Strength, toughness, item drops |
| **Mining** | Mine ores, stone, deepslate, and minerals | Strength, toughness, faster mining |
| **Fishing** | Catch fish and treasure | Luck, health, rare loot |
| **Excavation** | Dig dirt, sand, gravel, clay, and snow | Luck, toughness, faster digging |
| **Archery** | Shoot bows and crossbows | Crit chance, ranged damage |
| **Defense** | Take damage, block attacks, absorb hits | Toughness, health, damage reduction |
| **Fighting** | Deal melee damage with swords, axes, and other weapons | Strength, crit chance, damage output |
| **Agility** | Run, jump, swim, and move quickly | Speed, endurance, mobility |
| **Enchanting** | Enchant items and use enchantment bottles | Mana, wisdom, stronger enchants |
| **Alchemy** | Brew and use potions | Wisdom, health, potion power |

### Skill Details

<details>
<summary>Farming — details & tips</summary>

Train by harvesting mature crops, shearing sheep, and breeding animals. Farming often rewards steady, repeatable XP.

- Training tips: use tools suited for harvesting, replant crops promptly, and breed animals for burst XP.
- Typical benefits: improved health, faster crop yields, and regeneration-related perks as you level.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Foraging — details & tips</summary>

Train by chopping trees, gathering plants, and breaking leaf blocks. Foraging gains from repeated wood- and plant-gathering actions.

- Training tips: collect saplings and logs, use the correct axe, and harvest leaves for extra drops.
- Typical benefits: increased strength, better item drops, and damage mitigation at higher levels.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Mining — details & tips</summary>

Train by mining stone, ores, and deepslate. Mining rewards steady activity and benefits from efficient tools.

- Training tips: mine ore veins fully and use efficient pickaxes where allowed.
- Typical benefits: faster mining speed, increased toughness, and better resource gains.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Fishing — details & tips</summary>

Train by using a fishing rod to catch fish and treasure. Fishing can yield food, rare items, and XP.

- Training tips: fish near structures or at night for varied loot; keep multiple rods available.
- Typical benefits: improved luck, extra health benefits, and higher chance for rare catches.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Excavation — details & tips</summary>

Train by digging dirt, sand, gravel, clay, and snow. Excavation favors repeated digging actions.

- Training tips: focus on large deposits and use the correct shovel to maximize XP per action.
- Typical benefits: faster digging, increased luck, and improved toughness.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Archery — details & tips</summary>

Train by shooting bows and crossbows and landing hits on targets or mobs.

- Training tips: practice long-range shots and use different arrow types where applicable.
- Typical benefits: higher crit chance, better ranged damage, and accuracy perks.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Defense — details & tips</summary>

Train by taking damage, blocking attacks, and using defensive actions in combat.

- Training tips: use shields and tanking strategies in controlled fights to gain defense XP safely.
- Typical benefits: increased toughness, health boosts, and reduced incoming damage.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Fighting — details & tips</summary>

Train by dealing melee damage with swords, axes, and other close-range weapons.

- Training tips: chain-combo mobs and practice on varied targets to maximize XP gains.
- Typical benefits: improved strength, crit chance, and overall damage output.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Agility — details & tips</summary>

Train by running, jumping, swimming, and performing movement-based actions.

- Training tips: traverse varied terrain, use sprint-jump sequences, and engage in movement challenges.
- Typical benefits: increased speed, better mobility, and stamina-related bonuses.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Enchanting — details & tips</summary>

Train by enchanting items, using enchantment bottles, and combining enchanted gear.

- Training tips: gather enchantment resources and experiment with different enchant combinations to learn effects.
- Typical benefits: increased mana, wisdom, and stronger enchant outcomes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Alchemy — details & tips</summary>

Train by brewing potions and using them in active scenarios.

- Training tips: set up a brewing station, keep ingredients ready, and practice potion combinations.
- Typical benefits: improved potion potency, longer durations, and wisdom-based bonuses.

</details>

### Skill Progression

- Every skill levels independently.
- Leveling increases your stats and unlocks more powerful passive bonuses.
- Track your progress using `/skills`.
- Use `/stats` to see active attribute bonuses and modifiers.

### Commands
| Command | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/skills` | Open the main skills and progress menu. |
| `/stats` | View your detailed attribute stats. |
| `/<skill>` | View details for a specific skill (for example, `/mining`). |
| `/rank` | View the skill leaderboards and top players. |
