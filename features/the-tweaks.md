# Simple Voice Chat

Every players who have the `Simple Voice Chat` mod can talk to each other, without discord.

# One Mace

Only *one* mace can be crafted, it will make you the most wanted player if you own it !


# One Spear

Only *one* spear can be crafted, making your adventure harder.

# Sit

By clicking on a block, you can take a sit and chill.
